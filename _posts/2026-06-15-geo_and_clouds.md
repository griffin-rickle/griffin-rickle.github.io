Threw these together on a whim. Not sure what I'm working on next, need a bit more motivation I think. Both of these visuals are highly influenced by the new Boards of Canada album, Inferno. Enjoy!


# Geo
Threw this together from a picture of some lantana my wife planted in a pot on our back patio. Some pretty fun work with layer masks and geometry.
[![A visual art piece consisting of a geometric pattern overlaid on a picture of lantana](/assets/images/geo.png)](/assets/images/geo.png)

# Clouds
I was working on a cool radial effect emerging from the center of the image. It came together OK, but I didn't like the way Gimp was applying the rotataions at the center of the image. So I ran with what I had and made this cool image of a triangle overlaid on a picture of a cloud I took.
[![A visual art piece consisting of a triangle with radial inward lines towards a center circle overlaid on an image of a cloud](/assets/images/clouds.png)](/assets/images/clouds.png)
