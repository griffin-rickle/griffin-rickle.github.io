# Foreword
During one of my camping trips in northern PA, I took a photo of some mountain laurel beind some really lush grass. I've never seen another texture like it. Later, I drew some cool layered objects in different colors across the page. Some years later I combined them and came up with a cool visual image I call Evening Chamomile. The title is inspired by the following lyrincs in Deafheaven's song Glint:
> The midnight blue
> Of your calmness
> Evening chamomile
