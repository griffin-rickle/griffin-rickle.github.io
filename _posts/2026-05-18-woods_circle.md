# The Process
A couple of summers towards the end of my college career I was paid to camp and map carbon biomass across the Mid-Atlantic region of North America. Some of the areas we visited were these really cool and dense laurel plots, pictured below

[![A plot of mountain laurel somewhere in North Western Pennsylvania](/assets/images/2025-05-15-mountain_laurel.JPG)](/assets/images/2025-05-15-mountain_laurel.JPG)

I think that some of the textures in there are absolutely incredible. The actual process of creating the piece was actually pretty simple:
- Pick an area of the piece which is free of people
- Map it all to black and white
- Create the initial solid circle (I chose maroon)
- Use the lasso-select tool to create selections which roughly map to some of the paths within the black and white woods patterns
- Once a selection is created, delete that section of the solid circle
- Create a new layer
- Select all
- Shrink the selection to fit the circle
- Stroke the selection with the chosen color to make the rectangle.

In the future maybe I will go more in depth with the description of how to actually make the visual. For now, cheers!
